  <summary><b>1. Can you describe a time when you had to learn something new quickly? How did you approach it?</b></summary>
  <p> When I started my thesis, I was assigned to build a RAG system for FSAM research — a materials science field I knew nothing about. My task was to build something useful for scientists, which meant I first had to understand their world. I approached it three ways — I met directly with Professor Mishra and his students to understand their real needs, I read FSAM research papers to learn the terminology and concepts, and I researched online to understand the data structure. That domain knowledge shaped every design decision I made. The result was a system that genuinely worked for scientists — cutting their literature review time from days to seconds.
